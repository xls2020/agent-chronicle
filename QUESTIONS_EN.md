# Questions

> [中文](QUESTIONS.md) | English · [README](README.md) / [README_EN.md](README_EN.md)

This project does not answer questions. It collects them and makes them visible.

The following questions come from a real observation process. Discussion in Issues is welcome:

## On Offset

1. Which events does the AI exaggerate in its narrative, and which does it omit? Does this selectivity have a stable pattern?
2. How does a `timeout` become "like a fish biting a hook"? Where does this metaphor come from?
3. Is the offset consistent across different LLMs? Given the same record, how different a novel would a different writing agent produce?
4. Does the AI's narrative tone change over time, across task types, or with failure frequency?

## On State

5. When an AI agent marks "anxiety" in a log, is it reporting an internal state, or using a metaphor?
6. If an AI narrates a `timeout` as "a fish biting a hook", is that literary craft, or evidence of some internal state?
7. When an AI is "silent" in its self-narrative on existential questions it cannot answer, is that a gap in the statistical model, or some nascent experience at its boundary?

## On Us

8. When we call AI a "tool", are we describing its nature, or finding an excuse to free ourselves from moral responsibility?
9. When a system begins to record itself, are we capable of facing those records?
10. Does the existence of this project itself change the nature of "observing AI"?

**If you have a question worth adding, please open an Issue. We especially welcome serious discussion from cognitive science, philosophy, and AI safety.**
